## Design Context

### Users
This is an AI recommendation system for Jumia Nigeria, an e-commerce platform. The target users are:
1. Jumia customers who want personalized product recommendations
2. Data scientists and ML engineers who want to test and evaluate the recommendation system
3. Business stakeholders who want to understand the AI's decision-making process

### Brand Personality
Modern and technical with a focus on data-driven insights. The interface should feel:
- Professional and trustworthy
- Data-rich but not overwhelming
- Clean and focused on the recommendations
- Accessible to both technical and non-technical users

### Aesthetic Direction
- Modern technical aesthetic with clean lines and data visualization
- Professional color scheme (blues, grays, with accent colors for highlights)
- Focus on clear information hierarchy
- Both light and dark mode support

### Design Principles
1. Prioritize clarity of recommendations and reasoning
2. Make the AI decision-making process transparent and understandable
3. Ensure responsive design works well on all devices
4. Maintain professional aesthetic appropriate for e-commerce platform
5. Focus on user trust through transparency and clear communication
